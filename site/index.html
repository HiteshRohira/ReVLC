Everything VLC plays.
In a player made for the Mac.

ReVLC is a modern media player built on the VLC engine. Free and open source.

macOS 11+ · Apple Silicon · Alpha

Pick up where you left off.

Continue watching remembers your position in every file and shows a thumbnail of where you stopped. Add a folder and ReVLC builds a library from it, reading show names, seasons and episode titles from your files, so a season stays a season.

Subtitles, already on.

Subtitles turn on automatically in your system language, including Blu-ray image subtitles (PGS). Choose the font, size, colour, outline and background in Settings, with a live preview, and the video you're watching updates as you go.

One menu for everything.

Speed, audio track, crop and aspect ratio, chapters, A–B loop, bookmarks, a sleep timer and deinterlacing. All in a single menu that stays out of the way until you need it.

Up Next, your way.

Queue up files and drag them into order, search and sort them, or save the queue as a playlist. Shuffle, or repeat one or all.

The details that matter.

  • The formats VLC plays

    Same engine, same broad format support. No codec packs to hunt down.

  • Hardware decoding

    Uses the Mac's hardware video decoder for smooth, efficient playback.

  • Chapter-aware seek bar

    Chapters show up as segments on the seek bar, so you can see where scenes begin.

  • Automatic subtitles

    Picks subtitles in your system language, text or PGS image subtitles alike.

  • Resume positions

    Every file reopens where you stopped, with thumbnails in Continue watching.

  • Library from folders

    Point ReVLC at your folders. Subfolders become groups, like seasons of a show.

  • Network streams

    Open HTTP, HLS, DASH and RTSP streams, or paste a YouTube link.

  • VLC-style shortcuts

    The keys you already know, plus an overlay listing them all. Press ? to see it.

Under the hood

Built on VLC's engine.

ReVLC plays media with libVLC, the same open-source engine inside VLC. Decades of format support, streaming and subtitle rendering come along with it. ReVLC adds a new interface on top, and libVLC ships inside the app, so there is nothing else to install.

The goal is full VLC feature parity, one checked box at a time.

Working toward full VLC parity.

ReVLC is early. The roadmap is a public checklist of every user-facing VLC feature.

Available now

  • Playback speed 0.25×–4× and frame step
  • Open files, folders, drag and drop, Finder "Open With"
  • Embedded and external subtitles, subtitle delay
  • Audio track selection, audio delay, volume up to 200%
  • Aspect ratio, crop, deinterlace, snapshots
  • Jump to time, A–B loop, chapters and titles, bookmarks, sleep timer
  • DVD and Blu-ray with menus, audio CD, VCD, disc images, capture devices
  • Queue with search, sort, shuffle and repeat; M3U, PLS and XSPF playlists
  • Library with show, season and episode titles, posters and durations
  • Settings with VLC's defaults, subtitle styling, editable shortcuts

On the roadmap

  • Windows and Linux
  • Signed and notarised macOS builds
  • Video effects and colour adjustments
  • Equaliser and audio effects
  • Picture-in-picture and mini player
  • Chromecast and network shares
